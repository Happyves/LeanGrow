Inner cluster transition based raking may be doomed : we simply get pointed to clusters containg theorems for whch the hypotheses names are contained in the goal names of the theorems that did the pointing ; theorems that require these goal names in addition to other, names, which may be present in the local context, won't be discovered !
May be this can be solved by performing pointing as follows : instead of querying the query tree for clusters that have the goal-names as super-set, relax the matching requirement on Trie.CommonCount to it simply being above a ratio.
Alternatively, let depth of match (or number of names in common) be returned as score ; then weight from hyp to goal cluster would be summ of these scores
