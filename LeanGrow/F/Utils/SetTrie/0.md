To find pattern in a collection of large expressions, we could first derive the leaves of the expressions (const, bvars, lit, ...). Then we consider the leaves of the pattern, and apply as first trimming criterion the following: the set of leaves of the pattern should be a subset of that of the expression, hence if we store the expressions in a set-trie based on these leaves sets, the query may become efficient.
