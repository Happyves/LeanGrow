

With SetTrie clusters, adding sepcial names for `Type` and `\r` makes no sense.

Make sure to study reduction of types before getting names (instances may be replaced...)

For sink names, make a safer version that looks at names of parents of sink, in the case that sink generates no names. This could be the case in `(v1 : Type) (v2 : v1 \r 2+2=4) (h : \all x : v1, v2 x )`, where sink `h` won't yield constant names, but `v2` would yield `Nat.add` etc...
